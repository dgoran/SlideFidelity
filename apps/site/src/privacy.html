Privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026.

Who we are

SlideFidelity is provided by Open IT Systems, LLC, Orlando, Florida, USA ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Contact us at support@slidefidelity.com.

Your presentations

SlideFidelity analyzes the open presentation inside PowerPoint on your device. The content of your presentation is never uploaded to SlideFidelity or to anyone else.

What we collect

  • Account data. If you sign in: your email address, your sign-in sessions, your trial dates, and your plan and subscription status. Sign-in codes are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Payment status. When you subscribe, Paddle tells us your subscription and customer identifiers and the subscription's status and dates. We never receive or store your card or bank details.
  • Link checks. When you choose to check links, SlideFidelity sends only the web addresses (http and https links) found in your deck to our service, which requests each address and reports whether it works. We do not store or log these addresses. We keep only a count of checks per account and per network address to prevent abuse.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider processes your network (IP) address and basic request information to deliver the service and protect it from abuse. We use short-lived counters keyed to accounts and network addresses for rate limiting.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share your personal data for advertising.

Data kept on your device

The add-in stores a few things in PowerPoint's local storage on your computer: your sign-in session, your current plan, your settings (such as brand profiles and the show-machine font list), and choices like "Don't ask again". They stay on your device and are never sent to us. Signing out removes the session; your settings stay until you remove them or the add-in.

SlideFidelity Helper (optional)

SlideFidelity Helper is an optional, free app for PowerPoint on Mac and Windows computers. SlideFidelity works fully without it. If you install it:

  • It reads only the names of the fonts installed on your computer: system fonts, fonts you installed, and Microsoft Office's own and cloud fonts. It never opens your presentations.
  • It gives those font names, its version, and its status only to the SlideFidelity add-in on the same computer (https://app.slidefidelity.com), over the computer's own loopback address. Nothing it reads leaves your computer.
  • It sends nothing off your computer, except checking https://app.slidefidelity.com/helper/latest.json for a newer version. That request does not include your font names or anything from your presentations. Administrators can turn the check off. Updates are offered in the SlideFidelity pane; nothing installs automatically.
  • It installs the helper app, a login item that starts it when you sign in, and a certificate unique to your computer that is valid only for localhost, 127.0.0.1, and ::1 and trusted for website (SSL) use only. The key that could sign other certificates is destroyed after the certificate is created.
  • Uninstalling removes the app, the login item, the certificate and its trust, and the helper's files. See SlideFidelity Helper for details and uninstall steps.

Why we use your data

  • To provide your account, trial, and subscription, and to send you sign-in codes (performance of our contract with you).
  • To check links you ask us to check (performance of our contract with you).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (our legitimate interests).
  • To keep records required by law, such as tax and accounting records held by our merchant of record.

We send email only to deliver sign-in codes and messages about your account. We do not send marketing email without your consent.

Who processes data for us

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the website, the add-in, and our service and database.
  • Paddle.com is the merchant of record for purchases. It processes your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy.
  • Resend delivers sign-in code emails. These emails are sent from signin@openitsystems.com, an address of Open IT Systems, LLC.

These providers act on our instructions, except where Paddle acts as an independent controller for the purchase. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where data protection law requires it, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Sign-in codes: until used or 10 minutes, whichever comes first.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Rate-limit counters: deleted once their time window ends.
  • Account, trial, and subscription records: while your account exists. Deleting your account removes them.
  • Records Paddle needs for tax and accounting: kept by Paddle for the period the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. You can delete your account at any time from the Account section in SlideFidelity: this removes your email, sessions, and subscription records, and cancels an active subscription. For any other request, email support@slidefidelity.com. We will answer within 30 days. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information, and we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Sign-in codes are stored only as hashes, sessions are signed, and access to our systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you about a breach that affects you as the law requires.

Children

SlideFidelity is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes to this policy

We will post updates here with a new effective date and, for significant changes, tell signed-in users in advance.
