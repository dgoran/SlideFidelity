Install

In PowerPoint, open Home › Add-ins, search for SlideCheck, and select Add. SlideCheck needs PowerPoint for Mac 16.105 or later, PowerPoint for Windows (Microsoft 365, version 2601 or later), or PowerPoint for the web for every feature. Older versions show which features they cannot run.

Account and subscription

Sign in from the Account section in SlideCheck with your email address. You receive a 6-digit code. From the same section you can open your subscription page to upgrade, update payment details, or cancel.

Why some items cannot be fixed

PowerPoint does not let add-ins change fonts and colors inside charts and SmartArt, fonts set only for Asian or complex scripts, or content on slide masters. SlideCheck lists these so you can change them in PowerPoint.
